SECTION 3: SACRAMENT OF BAPTISM (Binyag / Pagbibinyag)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Taglish Aliases: Magpa-binyag, requirements sa binyag, ninong, ninang, pre-jordan seminar, bayad sa binyag, christening

SCHEDULE:
- Regular Community Baptism (Binyagang Bayan): Every Sunday at 11:30 AM.
- Special / Individual Baptism: Scheduled Tuesday to Saturday by advance booking.

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS (Mga Kailangang Dokumento):
1. Child's PSA Birth Certificate (original copy for verification + 1 clear photocopy).
2. Parents' Catholic Marriage Certificate (Kasal sa Simbahan).
   *Important Pastoral Clarification:* If the parents are civilly married or not married in church, the child is STILL FULLY ACCEPTED for Holy Baptism. Catholic Canon Law (Can. 868) states that no child is penalized for the marital status of the parents, provided there is hope of Catholic upbringing.
3. Parish Permit / Certificate of No Record (required only if the family resides outside Marigold Village / Muzon West territory).

GODPARENT GUIDELINES (Mga Patakaran sa Ninong at Ninang):
- Official Church Register allows a maximum of 2 principal godparents (1 Ninong and 1 Ninang). Additional sponsors may serve as honorary witnesses.
- Canonical Qualifications: Must be at least 16 years old, a baptized Catholic who has received First Communion and Confirmation, and living in accordance with the Christian faith.

PRE-BAPTISMAL SEMINAR:
- Held every 2nd and 4th Saturday of the month, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM at the Parish Hall.
- Attendance: Both parents and primary godparents must attend prior to baptism.

CANONICAL STIPENDS & DONATIONS:
- Regular Community Baptism: Suggested donation of ₱500 (covers candle, baptismal certificate, and maintenance of the sanctuary).
- Special Baptism: Suggested donation of ₱1,500.
- Financial Hardship Clause: In accordance with church canon law, no family will be turned away due to poverty. Speak privately with the parish secretary.

SECTION 4: SACRAMENT OF MATRIMONY (Kasal / Kasalan)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Taglish Aliases: Ikakasal, requirements sa kasal, bayad sa kasal, pre-cana seminar, CENOMAR, church wedding, tawag sa simbahan

LEAD TIME:
- Couples must coordinate and reserve at the parish office at least 6 MONTHS prior to the planned wedding date to complete canonical and civil paperwork.

DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS:
1. PSA Birth Certificate of Bride and Groom (issued within past 6 months).
2. PSA Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR, valid within past 6 months).
3. Baptismal Certificate with specific annotation: "FOR MARRIAGE PURPOSES ONLY" (issued within 6 months by baptismal parish).
4. Confirmation Certificate (Kumpil) with annotation: "FOR MARRIAGE PURPOSES ONLY" (issued within 6 months).
5. Canonical Interview: Conducted by the Priest-in-Charge at least 2 months prior to wedding.
6. Pre-Cana Seminar Certificate of Attendance (held through the Diocesan Family & Life Ministry or parish-accredited seminars).
7. Publication of Marriage Banns (Tawag sa Simbahan): Announced for three (3) consecutive Sundays in the territorial parishes of both bride and groom.
8. Valid Civil Marriage License from the Local Civil Registrar, or PSA Marriage Certificate (if already legally civilly married).

CANONICAL STIPENDS & SERVICES:
- Standard Parish Liturgy: ₱4,000 (includes priest, lighted church, basic liturgical sound, and marriage registration).
- Solemn Nuptial Mass: ₱8,000 (includes full liturgical lighting, wedding carpet, and altar flowers).
- Inquiries & Booking: Can be initiated online via the Sacralink portal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Taglish Aliases: Magpa-kumpil, requirements sa kumpil, confirmation schedule, ninong sa kumpil
