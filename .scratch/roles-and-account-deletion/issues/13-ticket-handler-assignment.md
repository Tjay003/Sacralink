# 13: Ticket handler assignment

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent
**Blocked by:** 05, 11, 12

**What to build:** A Church Admin can assign a church-lane ticket to one Priest or Volunteer of their church, and can take it back. **Assignment is what grants access** (spec decision 25): before it the Priest or Volunteer cannot see the ticket at all; once assigned they see it and are a handler (reply and change status); when unassigned they lose sight of it immediately. `conversations.assigned_to` and `is_ticket_handler` already exist after tickets 10a and 11; this ticket adds the write path and the UI.

Migration (next free number; append to `APPLY_ALL_MIGRATIONS.sql`):
- `public.ticket_assign(p_ticket_id uuid, p_assignee uuid) RETURNS void` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, row lock, `GRANT` to `authenticated`). Rules: caller is a Church Admin of the ticket's church (`is_ticket_handler` as the Church Admin); the Super Admin cannot assign (they only see escalated tickets; sending a ticket back is ticket 14, and a sent-back ticket cannot be assigned by them either); ticket is not `closed` and not escalated; `p_assignee` is a Priest or Volunteer currently assigned to that church (`assigned_church_id` or `church_id` equals the ticket's church, `role IN ('priest','volunteer')` or `is_priest`), not the requester, not deleted; `p_assignee = NULL` unassigns. Not allowed on `platform` lane tickets. Adds the assignee to `conversation_participants` if missing (so it appears in their list), writes a system note ("<name> assigned this ticket to <name>" / "unassigned"), touches `updated_at`. Reassigning or unassigning **removes the previous assignee's participant row** (unless they are the requester) and **deletes their unread notifications for that ticket**; their read access already ends through the live check in `can_access_conversation` (ticket 11), which is the authority, so no stale row can leak the ticket. At most one assignee at a time.
- Assignment does not change status by itself.

Web: an **Assign** control in the ticket header or info panel for the handler Church Admin: a picker of the church's Priests and Volunteers (reuse the staff list helper that already feeds the church staff views in `web/src/lib/supabase/profiles.ts`; exclude deleted users once ticket 07 lands) plus "Unassigned (Church Admin)". Show "Assigned to <name>" to everyone who can read the ticket, including the requester. Add `assignTicket(ticketId, userId | null)` in `web/src/lib/supabase/messaging.ts` and update `web/src/types/database.ts`.

**Staff removal (spec decision 37).** This ticket extends `public.cleanup_staff_assignments` (created in ticket 05 and also called by `anonymize_user` in ticket 07) so that, when someone stops being staff of a church (demoted, moved or deleted), every **church ticket assigned to them** is unassigned (`assigned_to = NULL`, their participant row removed, their unread ticket notifications deleted) and a system note is written ("<name> is no longer on staff; the ticket returned to the Church Admin"). The live predicate in ticket 11 already stops them reading at once, so there is no window. Their **own open platform tickets are not touched**: the Super Admin finishes them. A suspended assignee is still staff (suspension keeps the role), so nothing is cleaned up: the Church Admin reassigns, and the UI shows "Unassigned" whenever `assigned_to` is empty.

- [ ] A Church Admin can assign a church ticket to a Priest or Volunteer of the church and unassign it; the assignee can then see it, reply and change status, and no other Priest or Volunteer can even see it.
- [ ] After unassign or reassign the previous assignee can no longer read the ticket or its messages, it disappears from their list and counts, and their unread notifications for it are gone.
- [ ] A Church Admin cannot assign to someone outside the church, a Parishioner, the requester, or on a platform or escalated ticket; a Priest, Volunteer or the Super Admin cannot assign at all.
- [ ] A system note records each assignment; the requester sees who handles the ticket.
- [ ] A demoted, moved or deleted assignee loses handler rights at once and the ticket is unassigned automatically with a system note (it returns to the Church Admin); their own open platform tickets stay open for the Super Admin.
- [ ] When the ticket is escalated (ticket 14) the assignee and the Church Admin lose all access at once (list, messages, notifications) and `assigned_to` is cleared; after a send back the Church Admin can assign again.

**Test plan** (local `ticket-assignment.spec.ts`): RPC permission cases via `clientAs` (Church Admin yes; assignee, other staff, requester, Super Admin, other church's admin no), assignee gains visibility and handler rights (read, post and set status) and loses all of it on unassign (read returns nothing), the demoted-assignee fallback (demote through the service client, assert the post is rejected), and one UI happy path (assign from the picker, assignee replies). Five tests. Serialize e2e.
