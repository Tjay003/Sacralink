# 10b: Direct-message rule (matrix, creation function, legacy chats)

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent
**Blocked by:** 02

**What to build:** **Enforce the Direct-message matrix in the database (spec decision 27; the matrix is at the top of the spec).** Today a new Direct chat is built by the browser: it inserts a `conversations` row (`type = 'direct'`) and then `conversation_participants` rows, guarded by the policies in `036` (cases 2a to 2e: Parishioners and Volunteers can add Church Admins and the Super Admin, Church Admins add anyone they created the chat for, Priests add staff or a parishioner they share an appointment with), plus the restrictive policies in `059` and `061` (`volunteer_may_add_participant`, "Volunteers only message their own parish staff"). Replace all of that with one rule:

Migration (next free number; append to `APPLY_ALL_MIGRATIONS.sql`):
- `public.may_start_direct(_initiator uuid, _target uuid) RETURNS boolean` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `STABLE`, fixed `search_path`) implementing the matrix exactly: Parishioner and Volunteer never; Priest only to a parishioner who has an appointment with `priest_id = _initiator` and to a Church Admin of the Priest's own church; Church Admin to the Super Admin, to any Church Admin, and to a parishioner, Priest or Volunteer of their own church (never another church's parishioners, Priests or Volunteers); Super Admin to Church Admins only. "Own church" is `COALESCE(assigned_church_id, church_id)` on both profiles. Blocked or deleted accounts (spec decision 26) are neither initiators nor targets.
- `public.start_direct_conversation(p_target uuid) RETURNS uuid` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `GRANT` to `authenticated`, calls `assert_account_active()` and `may_start_direct`): returns the existing Direct conversation between the two people if there is one, otherwise creates the conversation and both participant rows in one transaction. It rejects with a readable error that says which route to use ("Raise a ticket instead", "Use the church staff channel", "Raise a platform ticket to reach the Super Admin").
- Tighten the policies so REST cannot bypass the function: the `conversations` INSERT policy no longer allows `type = 'direct'` for `authenticated` (same approach as tickets in step 4), and the `conversation_participants` INSERT policy no longer lets anyone add a different user to a Direct conversation (a participant may still not be added by REST; the function is the only way). Drop `volunteer_may_add_participant` and its restrictive policy. Channels, ministry groups, tickets and their policies are unchanged.
- **Existing conversations stay readable; legacy chats become read-only for Parishioners and Volunteers (spec decision 33).** Add `public.direct_is_legacy(_conversation_id uuid) RETURNS boolean` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `STABLE`, fixed `search_path`): true for a `type = 'direct'` conversation whose `created_by` could not start it today (`NOT may_start_direct(created_by, the other participant)`; when `created_by` is NULL, true if the matrix fails in both directions). Add a `RESTRICTIVE` INSERT policy on `public.messages`: reject a message in a conversation where `direct_is_legacy(conversation_id)` and the sender's role is `user` or `volunteer`, with the readable error "Please open a ticket". Church Admins, the Super Admin and Priests can still post; chats between two Church Admins or a Church Admin and the Super Admin, and chats whose starter could still start them (for example one a Church Admin started with a parishioner of their church), are not legacy. Rows, participants and earlier messages are untouched. Expose the flag to the UI (a column on the conversation list query or a small view) so ticket 12 can show the banner without guessing.

Web:
- `createDirectConversation` (`messaging.ts` ~815) becomes a thin wrapper over the function; delete its Parishioner, Priest and Volunteer pre-checks and `isVolunteerAllowedContact` (client) in favour of the function's errors. The contact pickers per role are ticket 12.
- Update `web/src/types/database.ts` (the function signatures and the legacy flag on the conversation row).
- The **contact pickers per role, the banner for legacy chats and the New Message routing are ticket 12**, which is blocked by this ticket because it reads `may_start_direct` and `direct_is_legacy`.

Ordering notes: blocked and deleted accounts (spec decision 26) are neither initiators nor targets; if ticket 07 has shipped, use `is_user_blocked` for that, otherwise ticket 07 adds the check when it lands. The Parishioner office shortcut (policy 2d of migration `036`) and the Volunteer allowance (`volunteer_may_add_participant`, `isVolunteerAllowedContact`) are removed here, in the database and in the client. Ticket 10a is independent of this ticket.

- [ ] **Direct-message matrix (spec decision 27):** through REST and through the UI, each initiator can start a new Direct chat only with the targets in the matrix and with nobody else; Parishioners and Volunteers cannot start any; a Priest cannot start one with a Volunteer, another Priest or the Super Admin; a Church Admin cannot start one with parishioners, Priests or Volunteers of another church (their own church's are allowed); the Super Admin cannot start one with anyone but a Church Admin. A direct `INSERT` of a `direct` conversation or of a participant row through REST is rejected for everyone.
- [ ] Existing Direct conversations stay listed and readable. Chats their starter could not start today become read-only for new messages from Parishioners and Volunteers (rejected by REST too), while Church Admins, the Super Admin and Priests can still reply; Church Admin to Church Admin and Church Admin to Super Admin chats, and chats whose starter could still start them, stay fully replyable for both sides.

**Test plan** (local `direct-matrix.spec.ts`; `clientAs` for RLS-subject calls, service client for assertions): a table-driven test of `start_direct_conversation` for every initiator role against every target role (allowed and rejected cells; own church versus another church for Priest and Church Admin; a Priest with and without a shared appointment); a direct REST insert of a Direct conversation or of a participant row rejected for each role; and the legacy rule: a seeded Direct chat a Parishioner or Volunteer started with the office is read-only for that Parishioner or Volunteer by REST but still replyable by the Church Admin, while a Church Admin to Church Admin chat and a Church Admin-started chat with a parishioner of their church stay replyable for both sides, and existing history stays readable. Needs a Super Admin, Volunteer, Priest and a second-church Church Admin: reuse the `support.ts` helpers (ticket 10a adds them if it ships first, otherwise add them here by lifting `tempUser` from `messaging-privacy.spec.ts`). Five or six tests. Serialize with other e2e agents; migrations to production from the main conversation only.
