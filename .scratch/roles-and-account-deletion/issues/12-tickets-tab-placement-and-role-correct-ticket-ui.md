# 12: Tickets tab placement and role-correct ticket UI

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent
**Blocked by:** 10b, 11

**What to build:** Fix the two client complaints ("ticket messages are not under Tickets" and "the status dropdown is hidden") and make the screen match the agreed rules. **Start by reproducing both as a real user**, per the repo rule for bugs: run the app against the local stack (`cd web; npx vite --mode e2e --port 5174`) as a parishioner who opens a ticket and as the Church Admin who answers it, and write the failing e2e first. The code reading below says what to expect.

Findings in `web/src/pages/messages/MessagingPage.tsx` and `web/src/lib/supabase/messaging.ts`:
1. **The Tickets tab matches every conversation.** `041` added `conversations.category DEFAULT 'general'` and `status DEFAULT 'open'`, so every row (direct, staff channel, ministry group) has a category. The Tickets filter (`filteredConversations`, ~864) is `c.type === 'group' || c.category || c.ticket_number`, which is true for all of them, and the enrichment in `fetchUserConversations` (~494 to ~500) keeps `category` for non-tickets. Ministry groups (`type = 'group'` without a ticket number) also match on the first clause.
2. **Tickets also show under All**, mixed with chats; nothing excludes them.
3. **The info panel treats any conversation with a category as a ticket** (`ticket_number || category`, ~1752), so "TICKET INFORMATION" appears on plain DMs.
4. **The status dropdown depends on `isStaff` (~198) and `ticket_number` only.** `isStaff` includes `volunteer`, so a volunteer sees the dropdown on **their own** ticket as requester, while a Church Admin sees nothing on a thread that lacks `ticket_number`. A parishioner who messages the parish office through the Direct "office gateway" (`036_restrict_parishioner_direct_messaging.sql`, `messaging-privacy.spec.ts` "get a reply") creates a Direct conversation with no ticket number, so staff see an inquiry in Direct with no status controls: the most likely way the client saw "ticket messages not under tickets and no status dropdown". Confirm in the reproduction. **Decided (spec decision 27): the shortcut is removed**; ticket 10b adds the database rule, this ticket changes the UI so the same conversation can no longer be started (existing ones stay as they are).
5. **"Query B" in `fetchUserConversations` (~357) lists church-wide group tickets for every staff member of the church**, which breaks the privacy rule (spec decision 25).

Changes:
- A `isTicket(conv)` helper (`ticket_number` present) used everywhere. Tickets tab = tickets only. **All, Direct and Staff exclude tickets.** (Parishioners currently see All/Direct/Tickets; keep the tabs.) Drop `category`/`status` enrichment for non-tickets.
- A `canHandleTicket(conv, profile)` helper that mirrors `is_ticket_handler` (ticket 11) from fields already on the row (`ticket_lane`, `escalated_at`, `assigned_to`, `church_id`, caller role). The DB is the authority; this only decides what to render. Handler sees the status dropdown (calls `updateTicketStatus`, shows the error on failure and reverts the local value). Everyone else who can read the ticket (the requester) sees the read-only badge (`getStatusBadge`).
- **Narrow the list to what the database allows** (privacy rule). Change "Query B" so church tickets are listed only for a Church Admin of that church and only while the ticket is not escalated; a Priest or Volunteer gets a ticket in the list only through the participant row that assignment adds (ticket 13) and loses it again when escalation removes that row (ticket 14), and the Super Admin only through the participant row added by platform creation or escalation. An unassigned Priest or Volunteer never sees a church ticket: not in the Tickets tab, not in the Tickets unread count or the sidebar Messages count, not in search, and a `?conv=<id>` link shows the normal "conversation not found" state. The requester always sees their own tickets.
- Composer: shown to the requester and the handler; (an escalated ticket cannot be opened by the Church Admin or the assignee: for the assignee it leaves the list, counts and search entirely; for the Church Admin it is replaced by the **stub row** below; a link shows "conversation not found"; the Church Admin gets the ticket back only if the Super Admin sends it back); locked with the existing closed notice for `closed` (the Reopen button logic is ticket 15, keep it working with the existing function meanwhile). Resolved keeps the composer. There is no "read-only because you are other staff" view any more: other staff cannot see the ticket.
- **Parishioner New Message path routes to New Ticket (spec decision 27).** In `MessagingPage.tsx` the New Message flow for a Parishioner (the contact picker and the "office gateway" wording around ~773 and ~1891, where "Parish Office" is offered as a Direct contact) no longer offers the Church Admin as a Direct contact; it offers **New Ticket** instead (reuse `canCreateTicket` and `ParishInquiryModal` with the parish selector) with a one-line explanation ("Messages to the parish office are tickets so the office can track and answer them"). The "Parish Office" Direct option elsewhere in the same file (the staff-facing labels at ~1891 and ~1901) stays for staff-to-staff. Existing Direct conversations a Parishioner already has with the office remain listed under Direct, readable and replyable. **The New Message contact picker for every role lists only the people that role may start a chat with** (matrix at the top of the spec; the database rule is ticket 10b): Parishioner and Volunteer see no contacts (a Parishioner is offered New Ticket; a Volunteer is offered the church staff channel and New Ticket); a Priest sees parishioners who share an appointment with them and the Church Admins of their own church; a Church Admin sees the Super Admin, all Church Admins, and the parishioners, Priests and Volunteers of their own church; the Super Admin sees Church Admins. The picker uses the same predicate the database enforces, so nobody is offered a person the database would reject. Check that every church has a staff channel reachable from New Message for Volunteers and Priests.
- **Escalation stub row (spec decision 32).** In the Tickets tab the Church Admin sees one content-free, non-clickable line per escalated church ticket of their church: "TCK-123 · Escalated to Super Admin" with the escalation date, loaded with `listEscalatedTicketStubs()` (the function from ticket 14, wrapped in `messaging.ts`). No selection, no thread, no composer, no unread count and no notification; it never shows for the assignee, the requester or any other role, and it disappears when the ticket is sent back (the ticket then appears as a normal ticket).
- **Legacy Direct chats (spec decision 33).** For a Parishioner or Volunteer, a Direct chat the database marks legacy (ticket 10b, `direct_is_legacy`) keeps its history readable but replaces the composer with a banner "Please open a ticket" and a **New Ticket** button (Volunteers also get a link to the staff channel). For Church Admins, the Super Admin and Priests the composer is unchanged. The UI reads the legacy flag the list query already carries (ticket 10b), so it never guesses.
- **New Ticket form states (spec decisions 34 and 36).** When the chosen church has no Church Admin (`church_has_admin`, ticket 10a), the form shows "This church has no administrator yet" and offers only **Report a concern about my church**; the church categories are hidden. A Parishioner always sees a "Report a concern about my church" route in the form.
- **Ask the Super Admin to review (spec decision 35).** The requester of an unescalated church ticket that has had no handler reply and is at least 7 days old sees an **Ask the Super Admin to review** button in the ticket header with a one-line explanation; it calls `requestTicketReview(ticketId)` (ticket 14), shows any database error inline, and afterwards the ticket reads "Escalated to Super Admin" for the requester. The database is the authority on eligibility; the UI only decides when to show the button.
- **Direct chat with a blocked user (spec decision 38).** When the list flag from ticket 07 (`other_participant_blocked`) is true, the composer is disabled with "This user is no longer available" and the history stays readable; it applies to deleted users now and to suspended users once ticket 19 lands.
- Opening a ticket from a link or notification (`?conv=<id>`) switches `activeTab` to `tickets`.
- Show the lane on the ticket header and in the info panel ("Parish office" / "Super Admin", and "Escalated" once `escalated_at` exists). Show "Follow-up to TCK-######" when `linked_ticket_id` is set.
