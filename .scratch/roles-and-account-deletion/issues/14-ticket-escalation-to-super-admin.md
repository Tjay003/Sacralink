# 14: Escalate a ticket to the Super Admin and send it back

Type: task
Status: ready-for-agent
**Blocked by:** 11, 12

**What to build:** A Church Admin, or the Priest or Volunteer the ticket is assigned to, can escalate a parishioner's ticket to the Super Admin when the church cannot solve it (an app bug, a complaint about the church itself). A Parishioner can reach the Super Admin directly only through the single "Report a concern about my church" category (ticket 10a, a separate ticket, not a church ticket), and church staff through the platform lane; escalation (by the Church Admin, the assignee, or by the requester after 7 days with no reply, below) is the **only** way an existing church ticket reaches the Super Admin, and until it happens the Super Admin cannot see the ticket (spec decision 25). The same ticket adds the way back: the Super Admin can **send a ticket back down** to the church with a required note (spec decision 31).

Migration (next free number; append to `APPLY_ALL_MIGRATIONS.sql`):
- `public.ticket_escalate(p_ticket_id uuid, p_reason text) RETURNS void` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, row lock, `GRANT` to `authenticated`). Caller must be `is_ticket_handler` for the ticket: a **Church Admin** of the ticket's church or the current **assignee** (a Priest or Volunteer); ticket is `church` lane, not `closed`, not already escalated and never sent back (`sent_back_at IS NULL`, so a ticket cannot bounce up and down); reason at least 10 characters. It sets `escalated_at = now()`, **clears** `assigned_to`, removes the participant rows of the assignee **and of the church's Church Admins** and deletes their unread notifications for the ticket (so both lose all access, including when they escalated it themselves; only the requester and the Super Admin keep access, spec decision 13), adds the Super Admin to `conversation_participants`, moves status `open` to `in_progress` if still open, and writes a system note ("<name> escalated this ticket to the Super Admin: <reason>", metadata `{event: 'ticket_escalated'}`). After this `is_ticket_handler` is true for the Super Admin and false for the Church Admin and the assignee, who both lose all access; the Super Admin can now read the ticket (before this they could not).
- `public.list_escalated_ticket_stubs() RETURNS TABLE(ticket_number text, escalated_at timestamptz)` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `STABLE`, fixed `search_path`, `GRANT` to `authenticated`, calls `assert_account_active()`): returns one row per church-lane ticket with `escalated_at IS NOT NULL`, **only to a Church Admin of that ticket's church** (every other role, including the assignee and another church's Church Admin, gets zero rows). It returns **only** the ticket number and the escalation date (the label "Escalated to Super Admin" is constant in the UI): no id, subject, category, requester, assignee, message count or last message. It is the only way the Church Admin learns of the ticket; no policy, view or column grant gives them row access (ticket 11 stays strict). A sent-back ticket drops out because `escalated_at` is cleared, and the Church Admin then reads it as a normal ticket again (spec decision 32).
- `public.ticket_request_review(p_ticket_id uuid) RETURNS void` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, row lock, `GRANT` to `authenticated`, calls `assert_account_active()`): **requester-triggered escalation** (spec decision 35). Caller is the requester (`created_by`); the ticket is a `church` lane ticket, not `closed`, not already escalated and never sent back (`sent_back_at IS NULL`); it is at least 7 days old (`created_at <= now() - interval '7 days'`) and has **no handler reply**, meaning no non-system message from anyone other than the requester. It then does exactly what `ticket_escalate` does (share one internal helper so the effects cannot drift: `escalated_at`, assignment and Church Admin participant rows cleared, notifications deleted, Super Admin added, status moved to `in_progress`), with the system note "<name> asked the Super Admin to review this ticket (no reply for 7 days)" and the automatic reason "No handler reply after 7 days". The Church Admin then sees only the escalation stub.
- `public.ticket_send_back(p_ticket_id uuid, p_note text) RETURNS void` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, row lock, `GRANT` to `authenticated`): caller is the `super_admin` and `is_ticket_handler` for the ticket; ticket is not `closed`; note at least 10 characters. Allowed only for (a) an **escalated church ticket**, or (b) a **platform ticket whose requester is a Priest or Volunteer** and whose category is `general` or `report_problem`. **Rejected** for `report_person` tickets and a Parishioner's `report_church` concern (both may concern the Church Admin) and for tickets whose requester is a Church Admin (`church_setup`, `user_deletion_request`, or any category: there is no one below them to send it to). Effects: sets `sent_back_at = now()`; for (a) clears `escalated_at` and re-adds the church's Church Admins as participants (the Church Admin sees the ticket again and is the handler; there is no assignee, escalation cleared it); for (b) sets `ticket_lane = 'church'` and adds the church's Church Admins as participants; in both cases removes the Super Admin's participant row and deletes their unread notifications for the ticket, and writes a system note ("<name> sent this ticket back to the Church Admin: <note>", metadata `{event: 'ticket_sent_back'}`) that the requester also sees. **Privacy interplay (spec decision 29):** a sent-back platform ticket becomes visible to the requester and the Church Admin and **stops being visible to the Super Admin**; this is the one way a Church Admin can see a staff member's platform ticket, and it is the Super Admin's explicit choice. Sent back tickets can never be escalated again. A sent-back ticket no longer counts toward the requester's 3 open platform tickets (it is a church ticket now).

Web: an **Escalate to Super Admin** button for the handler (the Church Admin, or the assigned Priest or Volunteer) in the ticket header or info panel, with a modal asking for the reason (text area, required) and a warning that the person escalating (the Church Admin or the assignee) will no longer see the ticket and sees it again only if the Super Admin sends it back. Show an "Escalated to Super Admin" badge and the escalation note to the requester (they see the status and the handler change, as agreed). The Super Admin, who has no church and is therefore not served by "Query B" in `fetchUserConversations` (`messaging.ts` ~357), sees the ticket through the participant row added here; check it appears under the Tickets tab. The requester sees an **Ask the Super Admin to review** button when the ticket is eligible (ticket 12 renders it; this ticket adds the `requestTicketReview(ticketId)` wrapper). A **Send back to church** button for the Super Admin (hidden on `report_person` tickets and on tickets a Church Admin raised) opens a modal with the required note and a one-line warning that the Church Admin will see the ticket. Show "Sent back by the Super Admin" and the note to the requester and the Church Admin. Add `escalateTicket(ticketId, reason)`, `sendBackTicket(ticketId, note)` and `listEscalatedTicketStubs()` (ticket 12 renders the stub) in `web/src/lib/supabase/messaging.ts` and update `web/src/types/database.ts`.

- [ ] A Church Admin can escalate a church ticket with a reason; the Super Admin then sees it under Tickets and can reply and change status.
- [ ] Before escalation the Super Admin cannot see the ticket; after it they can read, reply and change status.
- [ ] After escalation only the requester and the Super Admin can open the ticket: the Church Admin and the assignee can no longer read, reply to or change it (no rows, messages, counts, search or notifications) and `assigned_to` is cleared. The Church Admin's list shows only the stub line with the ticket number, "Escalated to Super Admin" and the date; the assignee gets no stub; other staff see nothing.
- [ ] A Priest or Volunteer who is not the assignee (they cannot even see it), a Parishioner, the requester and another church's Church Admin cannot escalate; the assignee can; a platform ticket or an already escalated one cannot be escalated.
- [ ] The escalation note is visible to the requester.
- [ ] The requester of a church ticket at least 7 days old with no handler reply can ask the Super Admin to review it, which escalates it with the system note; before 7 days, after any handler reply, on a closed, already escalated or sent-back ticket, or by anyone other than the requester, it is rejected.
- [ ] The Super Admin can send back an escalated church ticket and a Priest's or Volunteer's General Question or Report a problem ticket, with a note of at least 10 characters; afterwards the Church Admin sees and handles it, the Super Admin no longer sees it, and it cannot be escalated again.
- [ ] Send back is rejected for Report a person or abuse tickets and for tickets a Church Admin raised; a Church Admin, Priest, Volunteer or Parishioner cannot send back.

**Test plan** (local `ticket-escalation.spec.ts`): permission matrix via `clientAs` (Church Admin yes, assignee yes, other Priest/Volunteer, Parishioner, requester, other church's admin no), the Super Admin reading nothing before escalation, the handler hand-over (Church Admin read and post both rejected, while `list_escalated_ticket_stubs()` returns exactly the ticket number and escalation date to the Church Admin of that church and nothing to the assignee, another church's Church Admin or any other role, and the stub is gone after a send back; Super Admin post accepted, assignee reads nothing and `assigned_to` is null), the Super Admin seeing the ticket in the Tickets tab in the real UI, one happy path through the escalate modal, the review button cases (requester only; rejected before 7 days, after a handler reply, when closed, escalated or sent back; the effects match a normal escalation; age is set by updating `created_at` through the service client), and the send-back cases: both allowed kinds (escalated church ticket, staff platform ticket) change visibility as described, the two rejected kinds fail, re-escalation after send back fails, and the Super Admin's participant row and notifications are gone. Seven or eight tests. Serialize e2e.
